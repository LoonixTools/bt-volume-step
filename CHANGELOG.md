## v1.0.0

_2026-08-16_

Welcome to the very first release of bt-volume-step! It gives your Bluetooth headphones and speakers clean volume steps.

AirPods Pro only know 16 volume steps, so every swipe moves the volume by 6.25 %, and your desktop walks through numbers like these:

```
6 · 13 · 19 · 25 · 31 · 38 · 44 · 50 · 56 · 63 · 69 · 75 · 81 · 88 · 94 · 100
```

### Highlights

- One swipe, one clean step
- Learns every device on its own
- Follows Plasma
